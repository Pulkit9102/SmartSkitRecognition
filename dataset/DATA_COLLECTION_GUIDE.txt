SKIN DISEASE DATASET - DATA COLLECTION GUIDE
============================================================

RECOMMENDED DATASETS:
------------------------------------------------------------
1. HAM10000 (Skin Cancer)
   https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kmader/skin-cancer-mnist-ham10000

2. ISIC Archive (Melanoma)
   https://www.isic-archive.com/

3. DermNet (Various Conditions)
   https://dermnetnz.org/

4. SD-198 (Skin Diseases)
   Search: 'SD-198 skin disease dataset'


DATA COLLECTION GUIDELINES:
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Image Quality:
   - Clear, well-lit images
   - Focused on affected area
   - Minimal blur or obstruction

2. Quantity:
   - Minimum: 50 images per disease
   - Recommended: 100-500 images per disease
   - Professional: 500+ images per disease

3. Diversity:
   - Different skin tones
   - Various disease stages
   - Different body locations
   - Multiple age groups

4. Data Split:
   - Training: 70-80% of images
   - Validation: 10-15% of images
   - Test: 10-15% of images

5. Ethics & Privacy:
   - Use only publicly available or consented data
   - Remove any personal identifiers
   - Follow data usage agreements
   - Comply with medical data regulations

DISEASES INCLUDED:
------------------------------------------------------------

ACNE:
  Description: Common skin condition causing pimples and spots
  Severity: mild-moderate
  Locations: face, back, chest

ECZEMA:
  Description: Chronic condition causing itchy, inflamed skin
  Severity: mild-severe
  Locations: hands, face, elbows, knees

MELANOMA:
  Description: Serious skin cancer requiring immediate attention
  Severity: severe
  Locations: any body part

PSORIASIS:
  Description: Autoimmune condition causing scaly skin patches
  Severity: moderate-severe
  Locations: elbows, knees, scalp, lower back

ROSACEA:
  Description: Facial redness and visible blood vessels
  Severity: mild-moderate
  Locations: face, nose, cheeks

VITILIGO:
  Description: Loss of skin pigmentation in patches
  Severity: mild-moderate
  Locations: face, hands, around body openings
